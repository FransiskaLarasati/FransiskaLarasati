Leave balances

Counting starts 1 Oct 2026: everyone begins with 17 (12 AL + 5 PH). Every AL, EO, PH or SICK typed in the roster from 1 Oct on is counted here. Each sick day needs proof: a doctor certificate, or a manager approval letter, or a hospital bill.

Staff on contract (SC) get 12 days annual leave + 5 public holidays a year. Everyone on staff by 1 Oct 2026 can use it from 1 Oct 2026; people who join later can use it after 1 full year of service. “Left of 17” = 17 − AL − EO − PH taken. Daily workers (DW) get no EO, AL or PH. Hindu staff PH: Galungan ×2, Kuningan ×2, Nyepi. Catholic staff PH: Christmas ×2, Easter ×2, Nyepi.

Salary follows the contract. Service charge is paid on the 15th and split by grade: managers & supervisors 100%, pre-opening staff 75%, staff hired in 2026 50%.
